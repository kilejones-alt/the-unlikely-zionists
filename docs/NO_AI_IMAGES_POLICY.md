# Original-image-only policy

This project does not permit AI-generated or AI-altered imagery.

- Use only original photographs, original screenshots, scanned documents, archival artifacts, and other real visual source material with a recorded provenance.
- Do not use generative image models, re-creations, "enhancements" that alter documentary contents, synthetic backgrounds presented as photos, face swaps, imagined people, or substituted look-alike images.
- Typography, HTML, CSS, vectors, transitions, shaders, backgrounds, and text assistance are allowed as **graphic design**, not as a substitute for evidence or photographic imagery.
- An image must not be described as uploaded, approved, or published unless the exact original file has been verified in the project's deployed assets.
- When an image is missing, label that fact and ask for the source asset. Do not reconstruct it or infer a replacement URL.
- Record source, date, caption, copyright/usage clearance, and publication/subject consent as applicable before publishing identifiable people or private submissions.
- For historical headlines and allegations, preserve the original wording and date, attribute the claim, and provide any necessary correction or disputed-status context. Never silently edit a historic screenshot.
- All participant-provided images and submissions remain private until specific, independently documented publication approval.
