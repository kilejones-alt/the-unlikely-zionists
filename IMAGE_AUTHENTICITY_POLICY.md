# Image authenticity policy — The Unlikely Zionists

**Hard rule: NO AI-GENERATED IMAGERY.** This prohibition includes AI-generated people, faces, photographs, documentary stills, newspaper clippings, images offered as historical artifacts, and AI edits or composites of source photographs. Do not generate replacement imagery, silently recreate an inaccessible original, or add invented imagery.

Only use authentic, traceable photography, original screenshots, scanned artifacts and publisher/source imagery that the project owner has supplied or approved. Preserve original visual content, captions and provenance. A historical headline reproduced as an artifact is not automatically proof the headline was correct.

If a required photograph or screenshot is missing or provenance cannot be confirmed: **leave the image empty and ask the project owner which real image to supply**. Do not invent it. Do not guess identities, context, image dates, permissions, or participants' stories.

CSS-generated layout, typography, geometric shapes, motion, accessibility effects, and GPU visual treatments are allowed, provided they do not alter documentary truth or manufacture images. Existing and new story submissions remain private until secure infrastructure and permission workflows are complete.
